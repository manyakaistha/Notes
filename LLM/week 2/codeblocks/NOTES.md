# Week 2 numerical convention notes

- The transcript describes a decoder layer as approximately 4M (self-attention 1M + cross-attention 1M + FFN 2M), while its encoder shorthand is approximately 3M. With `d_model=512`, `d_ff=2048`, standard projection and FFN biases, and three affine LayerNorms, the executable formula and PyTorch `TransformerDecoderLayer` each count 4,204,032 parameters: two attention modules = 2,101,248, FFN = 2,099,712, and three norms = 3,072. Thus ~4M is accurate as a rounded total; exact PyTorch count is about 0.2M higher after biases and norms. The original Vaswani et al. paper gives 65M for the base model and a tied-embedding setup; it does not establish the transcript's ~4M as an exact per-layer value.
- The transcript's sinusoidal formula and 4,000-step warm-up are the original paper conventions. PyTorch does not provide a built-in Transformer sinusoidal encoding or learning-rate schedule, so the code implements the displayed equation directly rather than asserting library behavior.
- PyTorch BatchNorm1d computes training statistics over batch and sequence/spatial positions per channel. With a single observation per channel (`[1,C,1]`), it raises an error rather than acting as the identity; LayerNorm still normalizes each token across its feature dimension.
